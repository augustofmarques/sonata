# GNOME HIG Reference

**Role in Sonata:** Normative Foundation.

Use the existing GNOME HIG skill as the operational source. Sonata does not reproduce the HIG here.

## Sonata adopts from GNOME

- information architecture
- layout principles
- navigation principles
- interaction and behavior guidance
- accessibility guidance
- keyboard/focus expectations
- writing style
- content principles
- platform-conscious UX reasoning

## Sonata-specific interpretation

GNOME guidance has high structural and semantic authority but relatively little authority over Sonata's final visual identity. Sonata may use a different color language, typography, shape language, surface treatment and expressive motion while preserving the HIG-informed structure.

## Important integration rule

When a Sonata visual decision appears to conflict with GNOME, first determine whether the conflict is actually visual or whether it changes structure/behavior. Visual adaptation is normally allowed; semantic and behavioral divergence requires explicit justification.
