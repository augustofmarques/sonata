# Expression — Visual Emphasis & Hero

Sonata explicitly supports variable visual salience.

Levels:

- Neutral
- Subtle
- Moderate
- Strong
- Hero

Salience may use:

- size
- contrast
- color
- shape
- whitespace
- elevation
- motion

## Hero

Hero is a composition mode rather than a component. It may combine large typography, expressive color, geometry, illustration, motion, translucent layers and strong focal actions.

Hero treatment is appropriate when it improves focus or identity and should not overwhelm ordinary workflows.
