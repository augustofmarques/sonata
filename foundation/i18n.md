# Foundation — Internationalization & Localization

Sonata is designed to work across locales and scripts without assuming Latin-only content.

## Requirements

- Support text expansion and contraction.
- Support right-to-left layouts where applicable.
- Avoid relying on fixed-width labels.
- Keep navigation and hierarchy meaningful under localization.
- Use locale-aware numbers, dates, currencies and units.
- Preserve semantic typography when fallback fonts are required.

## Typography

The system may select locale-appropriate fallback families by script. Noto is an acceptable baseline fallback, but Sonata does not require Noto when a better locale-specific family is available.

Locale-specific families may differ in font family, weight, line-height, tracking, optical sizing and metrics — not just the base family — when required for equivalent legibility (D-224). See [`../expression/typography.md`](../expression/typography.md) for the concrete scale and locale-token naming this generalizes.

## Directionality

RTL should be handled as a structural transformation, not as a manually mirrored visual theme.
