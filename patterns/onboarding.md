# Pattern — Onboarding

Onboarding is the broader system surrounding First Use. See [`oobe.md`](oobe.md) (D-198–D-203) for the normative OOBE/First Use model this pattern sits inside.

It may include:

- initial orientation
- contextual education
- feature discovery
- progressive introduction
- help/revisit experiences

OOBE is the initial entry point.

Onboarding may continue throughout the product (D-201).

## Demonstrate rather than merely describe

When possible, onboarding should allow users to see or interact with the feature being explained.

## Avoid forced education

Users should be able to reach useful work quickly.

Advanced explanations should be available without requiring every user to consume them.

## Re-entry

Previously dismissed onboarding may be accessible again through help, settings or contextual discovery (D-202).
