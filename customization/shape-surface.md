# Customization — Shape & Surface

Shape and surface treatment are secondary-priority customization categories (see [Overview](overview.md)) — eligible for user/product control, but not required to be exposed everywhere.

## Shape

Users may be offered a shape personality (Geometric, Soft, Rounded, Expressive, Organic) rather than raw radius values. The application decides whether shape is user-facing or fixed by product identity. See [`../expression/shape.md`](../expression/shape.md) for the personalities, the shape scale and how components map to shape roles.

## Surface

Surface treatment (solid, tinted, outlined, elevated, translucent, glass, flat) and translucency are eligible customization capabilities but should be exposed selectively — not every context benefits from a user-selectable surface style. See [`../expression/surface-depth.md`](../expression/surface-depth.md) for the surface hierarchy and elevation model these choices act on.

## Constraint

Reduced-transparency accessibility preferences (see [Accessibility](accessibility.md)) override translucent/glass surface choices regardless of user or product preference.
