# Customization — Motion

Motion is a high-priority customization category (see [Overview](overview.md)) because it directly affects comfort and, for some users, physical wellbeing.

## Motion preference levels

- Full
- Reduced
- Minimal
- Off

Applications may expose fewer levels than the full set. See [`../expression/motion.md`](../expression/motion.md) for the underlying motion-level/priority architecture (Instant/Fast/Standard/Emphasized/Expressive, and the Functional → Decorative reduction priority) these preferences act on.

## Constraint

System-level reduced-motion requirements are an accessibility constraint (see [Accessibility](accessibility.md)), not merely a preference — they must be respected even when an application does not expose its own motion control.
